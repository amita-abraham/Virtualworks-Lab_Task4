# Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) reports to explore patterns between medicines, reported symptoms, patient demographics, and patient outcomes.

Using a sample dataset of 100 records, the analysis identifies frequently reported medicines, common symptoms, report seriousness, and monthly trends. The results are presented through data analysis and visualizations to make the patterns easier to understand.

**Note:** The dataset contains synthetic data created for learning and practice purposes.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Identify the most frequently reported medicines.
- Analyze the most common reported symptoms.
- Compare serious and non-serious ADR reports.
- Examine patient age and sex distributions.
- Track monthly ADR report trends.
- Analyze reported patient outcomes.

## 🛠️ Tools & Features Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Data analysis and summarization
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Data visualization
- Data formatting

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Most Frequently Reported Medicines
Analyzed the frequency of reports associated with each medicine to identify the medicines appearing most often in the dataset.

### 2. Most Common Symptoms
Examined reported symptoms to determine which reactions occurred most frequently.

### 3. Serious vs. Non-Serious Reports
Compared the number of serious and non-serious reports to understand the distribution of report severity.

### 4. Patient Age and Sex Distribution
Explored the age and sex distribution of patients included in the dataset.

### 5. Monthly ADR Report Trends
Analyzed the number of reports across different months to identify changes in reporting frequency over time.

### 6. Reported Patient Outcomes
Evaluated recorded outcomes, including recovery, ongoing recovery, and hospitalization, to summarize the outcomes represented in the dataset.

## 📁 Dataset Description

The dataset contains 100 sample ADR records with the following fields:

- Report ID
- Report Date
- Patient Age
- Patient Sex
- Drug Name
- Drug Status
- Reaction Symptom
- Seriousness
- Outcome

## 📈 Key Learnings

- Practiced analyzing healthcare-related data.
- Developed skills in summarizing and visualizing categorical data.
- Used Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts to identify patterns.
- Explored monthly trends and patient demographics.
- Learned to present healthcare data insights in a structured format.

## ⚠️ Limitations

This project uses synthetic data for educational purposes. The findings do not represent real-world adverse drug reaction rates and cannot establish that a medicine caused a particular reaction.

The frequency of reported reactions alone does not indicate the safety or risk of a medicine.
